# Midpoint Ltd Long Term Incentive Plan: rules extract (FICTIONAL)

This is a made-up extract for testing. It is not a real plan and says nothing about any real plan's rules. Clause numbers are for this fixture only.

**1. Definitions**
1.1 "Vesting Start Date" means the date stated in the Award Letter.
1.2 "Leaving Date" means the last day on which the Participant is employed by a Midpoint group company.
1.3 "Committee" means the Midpoint Ltd remuneration committee.

**4. Vesting**
4.1 An Award vests over 48 months from the Vesting Start Date.
4.2 No part of an Award vests before the first anniversary of the Vesting Start Date (the "Cliff Date"). On the Cliff Date, 25% of the shares under the Award vest.
4.3 The remaining 75% vests in 12 equal instalments, one on each date falling 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30, 33 and 36 months after the Cliff Date.
4.4 Unless the Award Letter expressly provides otherwise, vesting does not accelerate on a change of control or on any other event.

**5. Leavers**
5.1 A "Good Leaver" is a Participant who ceases employment because of death, ill-health, redundancy, or retirement agreed by the Committee, or whom the Committee determines in writing to be a Good Leaver.
5.2 A "Bad Leaver" is any Participant who ceases employment and is not a Good Leaver, including on resignation or dismissal for gross misconduct.
5.3 Unvested parts of an Award lapse on the Leaving Date unless the Committee decides otherwise in writing.
5.4 A Good Leaver may exercise vested Options for 6 months after the Leaving Date. Options not exercised by then lapse.
5.5 Vested Options held by a Bad Leaver lapse on the Leaving Date unless the Committee decides otherwise in writing.

**6. Settlement of RSUs**
6.1 Shares under a vested RSU Award are delivered through payroll no later than the last payroll date of the month in which the vesting date falls.

**7. Options**
7.1 The exercise price of an Option is the price stated in the Award Letter.
7.2 An Option expires 10 years after its grant date if not exercised or lapsed earlier.
