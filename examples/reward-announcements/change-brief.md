# Change brief (Job B test)

Midpoint Ltd. Fictional. For testing only.

## The change

The annual bonus plan moves to half-yearly payments. This brief is for the all-staff announcement, the FAQ and the manager brief.

- Effective date: 1 January 2027, the start of the 2027 plan year.
- Under the new plan, bonus is measured and paid for two periods: January to June, and July to December. First payment is for January to June 2027, paid with August 2027 payroll.
- The 2026 bonus is not affected. It is assessed on the old annual basis and paid with March 2027 payroll.
- Who is affected: the 214 employees on the corporate bonus plan, grades B3 to D, in London, Dublin and Amsterdam.
- Who is not affected: sales staff on the commission plan; employees on the executive plan.
- Budget: the total bonus budget is unchanged at GBP 1.9 million for 2027.
- Each half-year is measured on its own targets. A weak first half cannot be made up by a strong second half, and the reverse.
- Bonus target percentages by grade are unchanged.

Note from Reward: "The new timetable starts with the 2027 plan year, effective 1 April 2027, so payroll has time to set up the August run."
