# Midpoint Ltd: promotion criteria, Analyst (P2) to Senior Analyst (P3) (fictional)

Review window: the last two half-year review periods (2025-H2 and 2026-H1).

1. Scope: owns a workstream end to end, from request to delivery.
2. Ambiguity: shapes the problem as well as the solution, with little direction.
3. Influence: changes decisions outside their own team.
4. Mentoring: has helped at least one colleague reach a stated goal.
5. Sustained: performing at P3 level across both review periods in the window.

Not criteria: time in role, effort, attitude.
