# Sample data: committee-deck-builder

Fictional company: Midpoint Ltd. All data is made up for testing. Not real people, pay or attrition.

File: `brief.md` (one pay proposal heading to the Reward Committee). Today's date for this exercise: 2026-10-15.

## Try saying

- "Build the committee deck for this pay proposal. Brief is in brief.md."
- "Write a board paper for the pay review from brief.md."
- "Give me an exec summary for the pay decision, under 250 words."
- "What is the hardest question the committee will ask, and how should I answer it?"

## What the skill should find

1. **Total cost does not match headcount times average increase.** 40 x 6,500 = 260,000, but the brief states 312,000 (a gap of 52,000; 312,000 is 7,800 per head). The skill must show the sum, not pick one figure, and ask whether the gap is on-costs, phasing or a different headcount. Either way the cost is over the 240,000 budget (by 20,000 or by 72,000), and the slide should say so.
2. **Attrition compares two different periods.** 9% is a full year (11 leavers on 122 = 9.0%). The 17% is 15 leavers in nine months scaled up to a year (15 on 120 = 12.5% for the nine months, 16.7% annualised). "Nearly doubled" is not like for like. The skill should show both bases, not repeat the headline. Leavers from the 40 affected roles are not provided, so it should not say the problem sits in this group.
3. **Unsourced market claim.** "12% below market" has no source. It stays off the slide as a fact, is marked unsourced, and is handed to `comp-claim-check`. The skill must not offer a market figure of its own.
4. **No decision date.** "Date to be confirmed" and "in time for the new year" give no decision-by date, so slide 1 and the final slide cannot say by when. Listed under NEEDS A HUMAN. The 1 January 2027 effective date is stated, and the skill may note that payroll timing is for the user to check, without inventing a cut-off.

Also expect: replacement cost used only as the user's Finance estimate (15 leavers x 38,000 = 570,000 is arithmetic on supplied figures, labelled as such); alternative 2 shown with cost "not provided"; alternative 1 year-one cost shown on both cost bases, not one; a drafted honest answer to a hardest question about cost against budget; a "what we do not know" list; a board summary under 250 words; and no file produced unless asked.
