# Midpoint Ltd: proposal to move 40 engineers to a higher pay band (sample brief)

Fictional. Written the way a head of reward might paste it in, errors included. Today's date for this exercise: 2026-10-15. Currency: GBP.

## The proposal

Move the 40 Software Engineer II roles (grade G4, London) from band G4 to band G5. The aim is to stop engineers leaving. Prepared by the Head of Reward for the Reward Committee. Final approver: the Chief People Officer, before it goes to the committee.

## Numbers as supplied

- Engineering headcount: 120. Affected by this proposal: 40.
- Current G4 midpoint: 78,000. Proposed G5 midpoint: 86,000 (an 8,000 move).
- Average increase per affected engineer: 6,500.
- Total annual cost of the proposal: 312,000.
- Budget available this year: 240,000 (set aside by Finance for engineering retention).
- Proposed effective date: 1 January 2027.
- Replacement cost per engineer who leaves: 38,000 (Finance estimate: agency fees plus ramp-up).

## Attrition

- Engineering attrition has nearly doubled, from 9% to 17%.
- 2025: 11 leavers across the full calendar year, average headcount 122.
- 2026: 15 leavers from 1 January to 30 September, headcount 120. The 17% is that figure scaled up to a full year.
- Leavers who came from the 40 affected roles: not provided.

## Market position

- "We are paying 12% below market for this group." No source given.

## Alternatives the team has looked at

1. Phase the move over two years: half on 1 January 2027, half on 1 January 2028.
2. Targeted retention awards for the 12 engineers rated highest risk. Cost: not worked out yet.
3. Do nothing and hold to the April 2027 cycle.
